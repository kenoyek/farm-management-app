# Formula specifications — model version 1

Use decimal arithmetic for money/quantities; round monetary display to 2 places, never round intermediate forecasts. Counts in actual records are nonnegative integers. Rates are explicit fractions in [0,1]. Reject NaN/infinite, invalid dates, negative prices, zero denominators and incompatible units. Tolerances: inclusion 1e-6 percentage point; nutrient comparisons 1e-7 in declared unit; solver feasibility 1e-7 after rescaling. Unknown is null, never coerced to zero.

## Feed
For percentage p_i, x_i=p_i/100. Require Σp_i=100 before valid results. Nutrient n=Σx_i*n_i; if any positively included ingredient lacks n_i, n is unknown and compliance is unknown/fail to release. Price/kg=Σx_i*c_i, batch cost=price/kg*batch kg, bag cost=price/kg*bag kg, ingredient kg=x_i*batch kg. Complete dry matter contributions rather than reproduce missing source formulas. Prices are historical snapshots, not live quotations. Compare each known result to explicitly defined min/max; do not infer min/max from the scalar workbook table.

LP: min cᵀx, Σx=1; l≤x≤u; nutrient minima/maxima; stock-derived upper bound stockkg/batchkg; fixed amounts are equality bounds. Verify species, stage, nutrient basis, required laboratory/verification status and all constraint data before solving. Use HiGHS, preserve input/model/solver versions, status and primal residuals. Infeasible is not an optimum; no automatic safety relaxation. Binding constraints are solver-evidence only. Approved immutable recipe version requires a nutritionist decision and audit.

## Layers feed
For each week opening B, ration g, price P, mortality m: feed=B*g*7/1000 kg; expected deaths=B*m; closing=B-deaths; cost=feed*P; next opening=closing. Baseline week1: 500*10*7/1000=35kg; 2.5 expected deaths; 497.5 closing; ₦10080. Feed uses opening-stock approximation; an alternative daily survival model must have a separate version. Do not silently replace the baseline. Full-cycle forecasts require supplied stage-specific ration schedules.

## Eggs
Period gross eggs=opening hens*lay fraction*days (legacy opening-stock approximation). Cracked=gross*crack fraction; saleable=gross-cracked; tray equivalents=saleable/tray size. For actual integer eggs: complete trays=floor(saleable/size), remainder=saleable % size. Expected mortality=opening*m; closing=opening-deaths. New monthly periods use actual calendar dates including leap years; legacy scenario explicitly uses 28 days. Actual hen-day%=100*eggs/sum daily live hens (not month-end hens*days). Revenue=saleable eggs*price/egg, or explicit tray/remainder pricing; not both. Source first-month examples are recorded in WORKBOOK_ANALYSIS.md.

## Broilers
ADG=(final average weight-initial average weight)/elapsed days. Biomass=count*average kg. FCR=feed kg/live-weight gain kg; identify cohort, deaths/culls, partial harvest biomass and time window, reject nonpositive gain. Revenue=live kg*price/kg or birds*price/bird; processed yield is separate. Cost includes chicks, feed actually consumed, medication, allocated labour/utilities and harvest/processing. Cost/bird and break-even price use saleable count/biomass. Gross margin=revenue-attributable costs; not cash collected.

## Catfish
Biomass=estimated live count*sample average grams/1000; daily feed=biomass*feed-rate-percent/100; SGR=100*(ln(final g)-ln(initial g))/days, strictly positive weights/days. Survival estimates and sample dates/sizes must be visible. Transfers debit/credit in one transaction. Separate harvest product yields and processing costs. Feeding rate and water-quality limits must be species/system-specific approved inputs, not invented constants.

## Inventory and finance
Inventory balance=Σsigned canonical-unit movements, with distinct locations/lots and unique source-event idempotency keys. Receiving transfers money to inventory assets, not automatically consumed-feed expense. Issue to production recognizes cost through an explicit valuation policy (weighted-average first release), retaining historical prices. Balanced journal debits=credits; revenue, receivable and cash collection are distinct postings. Sales/stock movements use one transaction; reject negative stock under concurrency via locks. Forecasts never post operational ledger events. Saved scenario versions are immutable, comparisons vary assumptions only.


## Broiler planning — daily linear targets v1

A placed batch's original count/weight/date anchor its immutable plan. Feed days cover ages [placement age, harvest age), and the harvest date is placement date + the supplied age difference. Interpolate weight linearly between the original placement weight and explicitly supplied/cited age targets, including a harvest-age point; never infer a universal strain curve. Daily feed = expected opening birds × stated ration g/day / 1000; expected deaths = opening birds × stated daily mortality fraction; closing = opening − deaths. Carry Decimal(precision 40) values without display rounding between days. Missing rations/mortality/prices are unknown, not zero; unknown mortality makes later population-dependent outputs unknown. Monetary outputs round to four places, weights/kg to six and expected counts to eight for reports. Feed and chick costs are separate forecast components. Gross live value uses one explicit basis: closing birds × price/bird, or closing birds × target g /1000 × price/live kg. No FCR, total cost, processed yield, margin or cash is inferred. Guide temperatures are user-supplied paired bounds, not automatic scientific thresholds.


## Broiler declared-budget/yield economics v1

Use the exact original linked daily-plan accumulations before display rounding. Batch cost components are original opening count × chick price, full planned feed cost and eight explicitly sourced additional batch totals. Known subtotal sums known components only; total is withheld unless all ten components are known and the user confirms the declared complete allocation boundary. Selling quantity is expected survivors for a bird sale, expected survivors × harvest g / 1000 for live kg, or that live biomass × supplied saleable processed-yield fraction for processed kg. Exactly one basis applies; all survivors are assumed sold at plan harvest, with no partial/by-product allocation. Revenue = quantity × stated price; projected margin = revenue − total; margin % = 100 × margin / strictly positive revenue. Unit cost/break-even = total / strictly positive corresponding quantity. Missing values propagate; explicit zero produces zero revenue/quantity but never division by zero. Decimal precision 40/HALF_UP; round output money to four places, kg to six, birds to eight and percentage to six. Currency inherits the original plan with no conversion. Compare two to four exact whole-batch versions in one original currency, subtracting four-place saved economic outputs; missing deltas remain unknown. Results describe the declared forecast budget, not actual costs, cash or ledger profit.


## Broiler measured-loss cohort biomass v3

A current loss weight must cover the exact positive whole-bird count and effective daily revision or original active cull. No per-bird multiplication, forecast curve or sample-derived mass fills a missing loss. Any effective daily revision change invalidates its previous weight claim, even if the count is unchanged; a new review must confirm the current source. Latest explicit-null reviews mean unknown and never fall back to an original cull weight. Ignore superseded and inactive/voided loss evidence. Complete death kg is the sum of current measured daily-loss group totals, or unknown if any positive death count lacks current mass. Complete cohort gain = estimated closing live biomass + complete death kg + current measured active cull/live-harvest kg − original placement biomass. Unknown loss/cull mass withholds gain/FCR. FCR = complete recorded feed / strictly positive cohort gain, retaining complete-day and representative remaining-sample timing gates. Carry Decimal precision 40, output kg/gain/FCR to six places. This includes growth subsequently lost to death/cull; it is not saleable-weight economic conversion. Review positions change loaded-state CAS but not the feed/actual activity window.


## Whole-harvest measured processing v1

The fixed `whole-harvest-no-added-mass` scope is the reviewer's declaration that the entire group was owned and unsold at processing, outputs are distinct (without nested carcass/cut counting), and no added mass or mixed harvest is included. An unpriced source alone establishes none of those facts; priced live sales, including zero-priced sales, are ineligible. Use the original immutable whole-harvest live kg denominator, never a forecast or sampled weight. Sum known outputs as a subtotal; complete output requires all line weights. Primary kg/yield require every primary weight; dressing yield is primary/original-live kg only for whole-dressed form. Intended-sale kg/yield require all intended weights and no unassessed classifications; a known intended subtotal is separate. Measured residual may be zero or unknown and must not repeat outputs. Reject known outputs + known residual > original live kg. Unreconciled kg = live input − complete output − measured residual, or unknown if incomplete. A positive difference is not inferred waste. Decimal precision 40; kg six places, fractions eight. Processing revisions/withdrawals never change original animal counts/live harvest weights, cohort FCR, feed windows or forecast/financial values.


## Source-linked broiler product stock v1

A receipt takes exactly one current known intended output's full measured kg, once, under the supplied owned/available/no-prior-disposition assertion. Available kg = active received kg − active sold kg − active measured loss kg, separately by immutable receipt lot. A void removes the erroneous original effect, preserving both rows; no physical return is inferred. Enforce nonnegative balances under the common unit lock and loaded product/source state. Carry kg to six places. Sale value = measured dispatched kg × explicit price/kg, Decimal precision 40 / half-up to four places, in the original posting currency. Reject amounts ≥10^16 (numeric(20,4) limit). Sum active sale values only within each original currency, never convert or subtract unknown costs. Zero prices remain distinct from missing prices. No per-bird product count, COGS, cash, actual margin or live-animal/FCR conversion is inferred.


## Reviewed incurred batch costs and explicit lot allocation v1

Use ten non-overlapping actual batch-attributed category totals in one explicitly supplied original currency: placed chicks, consumed-feed valuation, health, litter, labour, utilities, transport, processing, overhead and other. Known subtotal sums known four-place amounts, preserving explicit zero. Complete declared incurred cost requires all ten known plus the reviewer's completeness assertion; it remains an assertion rather than financial-ledger verification. No source workbook, recipe, purchase or forecast price supplies actual consumed cost.

Each selected active receipt lot receives an explicit evidenced nonnegative cost total A. Distinct lot allocations may not exceed the known incurred subtotal; no joint-cost share, sales-value weighting or cost for omitted lots/live birds is inferred. For original received W>0, active sold S, active measured loss L and remaining R, verify W=S+L+R. Display allocated cost/received kg as A/W to eight places. Compute sold kg cost C_s=round4(A*S/W), lost kg cost C_l=round4(A*(S+L)/W)−C_s, remaining kg cost C_r=A−C_s−C_l. This cumulative sold-then-loss precision convention retains exact four-place conservation even for rounding traps; do not multiply by a display-rounded unit cost. Sum all C_s+C_l+C_r and unallocated known cost to exactly the known incurred subtotal. Decimal precision 40, HALF_UP, kg six places, money four and displayed allocated unit cost eight.

Review snapshots freeze exact operational/processing/product evidence and source currencies. Record live/product sale values separately by original currency without conversion or netting into a margin. These cost attributions are managerial review outputs, not financial COGS, weighted-average feed inventory valuation, expense/cash/journal postings or realized profit. Later source changes mark a saved review historical without recomputation against new quantities. A new full cost version requires explicit current-source review.
